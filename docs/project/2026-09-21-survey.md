# 2026-09-21 parser survey

First survey run since 2026-09-16. Recorded per `parser-gap-workflow.md` step 1
after four parser changes landed without a survey between them: #338 (#337),
#339 (#333), #340 (#332) and #341 (#330).

## Why this run exists

The workflow requires re-running the discovery survey after any adapter change,
because the survey reports only the _first_ parse error per file: fixing one gap
can expose another that was masked behind it. Those four changes were verified
against the unit corpus and differential binaries, but never against the
reference corpus. This run closes that gap and establishes the post-change
baseline.

Six repositories from `corpus.md` checked out as siblings under `CORPUS_ROOT`

## Consequence for gap discovery

The corpus is exhausted as a source of new parser gaps at these revisions. The
gaps fixed in this session (#330, #332, #333, #337) were found by adversarial
probing of the Zsh grammar, not by corpus evidence, and the remaining open gaps
(#331, #324, #307) came the same way.

`parser-gap-workflow.md` step 2 prioritizes by corpus evidence. With no corpus
failures left, priority has to come from elsewhere: either expanding the corpus
under #138 / #132, or continuing to probe the manual for constructs the corpus
does not exercise. Recording that explicitly so a future run does not read
"0 failed" as "no work to do".
