## Known limits of this fix

`${#*:#x}` and `${#@:#x}` are valid Zsh and remain rejected.
The retry masks the length `#` into the parameter name, and `_*` and `_@` are not names, so the mask cannot parse.
Both keep the verdict they have on `main`, so nothing regresses; they are recorded in the PR rather than silently left out.
